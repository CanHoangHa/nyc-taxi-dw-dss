# Business Requirements

## Business problem

Analyze NYC Yellow Taxi demand and eventually forecast day-ahead pickup demand
for each taxi zone and hour.

## Intended users

- Taxi fleet or operations planners
- Analysts studying spatial and temporal pickup demand
- Decision makers prioritizing taxi availability for the next day

## Analytical questions

- How does pickup demand vary by month, day of week, and hour?
- Which boroughs and taxi zones record the most pickups and drop-offs?
- Which zone-hour combinations consistently exhibit high pickup demand?
- How does predicted demand compare with the relevant historical baseline?
- Where and when is next-day pickup demand expected to be high?

## Decision supported by the DSS

The DSS will support the decision of which taxi zones and hourly periods should
receive prioritized taxi availability for the following day. It will not claim
to optimize routes, estimate unmet demand, or calculate profit.
